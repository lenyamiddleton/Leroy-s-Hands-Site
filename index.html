Our Story

Built on compassion,
rooted in community

Leroy's Hands was founded with a simple belief: every person deserves to age with dignity, surrounded by genuine care and a true sense of belonging. What started as one man's vision has grown into a trusted home for residents and their families.

Our dedicated team works together to create an environment where independence is celebrated, and every individual's needs are met with patience and expertise. Leroy's Hands is providing home base stability for people in need of a structured living environment. This independent living environment targets veterans and/or people experiencing homelessness, and temporary housing displacement. Leroy's Hands offers long term and short-term housing.

Leroy's Hands will also focus on giving back to the community in which we strive to improve. We will be structured to encourage positive growth and seek accomplishments throughout our programs with a volunteer program that includes various opportunities for the community to grow.

What We Offer

Comprehensive care for
every stage of life

Our services are tailored to meet each resident's unique needs — from independent living assistance to comprehensive daily support.

🤲

Personal Care Coaching

Daily support with bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility to help residents maintain their independence and dignity.

Medication Management

Nutritional Dining
